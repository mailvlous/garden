set of logically related tasks performed to achieve a defined business outcome

- two important characteristics:
    - it has customers, either external or internal to a firm
    - it crosses organizational boundaries, i.e occurs accross or between organizational sub-units


#### business process
- kumpulan aktivitas yang saling terkait menghasilkan suatu layanan atau produk bagi organisasi internal atau eksternal(stakeholders)
- memiliki input dan output dan aktivitas yang terukur
- beberapa dapat meliputi satu atau lintas beberpa area fungsional

A business process represents a continuous series of interconnected activities aimed at producing a valuable product or service for an organization, its business partners, and its customers. This process relies on three
essential components:  
- Inputs: These encompass the materials, services, and information that flow through the process and undergo transformations as a consequence of the activities within the process.  
- Resources: People and other resources form the backbone of process activities, performing various tasks to facilitate the progression of the process.  
- Outputs: Outputs are the ultimate result of the process, ending in the creation of a specific product or service that is ready to be delivered to the intended recipient.  

*contoh bp:  
ordering goods from supplier
- customer: user of the good  
    involved parties: purchsing, receiving, account payable, supplier org
- developing new product
- creating a marketing plan
- processing a insurance claim, etc


### aktivitas proses organisasi
#### proses utama, pengelolaan dan pendukung
- management->process provide direction, roles and practice
- core process generate value as they are directly linked to external customers
- support process provide resources to be used by other process

#### aktivitas kunci
- menghitung process utama
- menghitung batas/boundaries/scope
- menentukan relevansi
