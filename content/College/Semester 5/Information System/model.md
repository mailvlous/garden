are abstractions from real world phenomenon, developed for purpose of reduced overall complexity.  
a model integrates information and document only relevant aspects of the real world.  
model are being developed:
1. in a specific modelling subject
2. for specific target audience
3. with a specific modelling purpose

#### elemen penting dalam  suatu model proses:
- what do we need to do and when  
    - activities, events
    - manual/automated
- what we need to work on
    - input/output artifacts to activities
    - physical/electronic
- who does the work
    - resource that performs activities
    - human/software

the result:
- conecptual process model
- exeutable process model
